OPEN CITY

A fast open-world action game. Own the streets, the sea front and the skyline.

About the game

Open City is a sunlit coastal metropolis with glass towers, quiet suburbs, parks, plazas and a palm-lined beach. Walk, run, ride a bicycle, jump on a motorbike or steal a car, bus or taxi. Day turns to night and the whole city lights up.

Your mission

The Red Gang has taken over the streets. Every gang member wears red. Take them down for cash and finish each mission for a big bonus. Stay away from civilians and police: hurting them raises your wanted level.

Controls - PC

  • W A S D move / drive
  • Mouse aim, Click or Space attack / brake
  • Shift run, E enter / exit vehicle
  • Q or 1-5 change weapon, H horn
  • Esc pause, M sound

Controls - Phone (landscape)

  • Left joystick move / steer / throttle
  • Drag on right side to aim
  • FIRE attack (hold and drag to aim), BRAKE in car
  • ENTER get in or out, GUN switch weapon, RUN sprint

Features

  • Animated people with arms, legs, hair styles and faces
  • Cars, sports cars, taxis, SUVs, vans, buses, motorbikes and bicycles
  • Fists, bat, pistol, SMG and shotgun
  • Wanted level with police response
  • Living traffic, pedestrians, day and night cycle
  • Pickups, minimap, explosions and burning wrecks
